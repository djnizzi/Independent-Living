## 2. Simulated interview evidence

### P01 — Anna (simulated)

- Context: 78, widowed, lives alone; uses a smartphone for WhatsApp and photos of her grandchildren; fears falling at night but rejects a safety bracelet.
- Quotes:
  - “Il braccialetto lo porta chi è malato. Io non sono malata.”
  - “Se mia figlia vede quando apro il frigo, mi sento in prigione.”
  - “Una luce che si accende da sola quando mi alzo, quella sì che mi servirebbe.”

### P02 — Franco (simulated)

- Context: 74, lives with his wife, former technician, interested in technology and already has a smart thermostat; wants to understand what devices do.
- Quotes:
  - “Voglio sapere cosa registra e dove va a finire.”
  - “Se una cosa decide da sola senza dirmelo, la stacco.”
  - “Mi piacerebbe un riepilogo dei consumi, non qualcuno che mi controlla.”

### P03 — Lucia (simulated)

- Context: 83, lives alone in the countryside; low digital familiarity, reduced hearing, daily visits from a neighbor; medication reminders are a possible support need.
- Quotes:
  - “I bottoni piccoli non li vedo e le voci veloci non le capisco.”
  - “Le medicine me le scordo, questo sì.”
  - “Non voglio disturbare mio figlio, lavora.”

### C01 — Marco (simulated)

- Context: 52, Anna's son, works full time, calls her twice a day, and experiences ongoing anxiety.
- Quotes:
  - “Se non risponde al telefono, mi parte il panico.”
  - “Non mi serve vedere tutto, mi serve sapere che sta bene.”
  - “Mi sento in colpa a controllarla.”

### C02 — Elena (simulated)

- Context: 47, Lucia's daughter and a nurse; manages treatment and medical appointments remotely.
- Quotes:
  - “Gestisco tutto su un foglio Excel e mille promemoria.”
  - “Vorrei che mia madre decidesse cosa condividere con me.”
  - “Gli allarmi falsi sono peggio di nessun allarme.”

### C03 — Ioana (simulated)

- Context: 39, part-time live-in caregiver five days a week; coordinates with the family through WhatsApp.
- Quotes:
  - “Nei giorni in cui non ci sono, nessuno sa se ha mangiato.”
  - “La famiglia mi chiede tutto per messaggio, è stancante.”
  - “Lui si arrabbia se sente che lo controlliamo.”

## 7. Journey: Anna and Marco

| Moment | Anna | Marco | Possible service opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Gets up and makes coffee; feels calm. | Wonders whether she is okay; feels anxious. | Share a “routine regular” signal only if Anna permits it. |
| Day | Goes out and shops; remains independent. | Works and calls; is distracted. | Avoid unnecessary notifications. |
| Evening | Watches TV and feels lonely. | Calls; no answer triggers panic. | Suggest a video call to Anna before alerting Marco. |
| Night | Gets up in the dark and fears falling. | Sleeps and feels calm. | Light the path; alert only for a real event. |
| Unexpected event | Falls and cannot get up; feels afraid. | Receives an alert and responds. | Gradual escalation: ask Anna first, then alert the caregiver as previously agreed. |
